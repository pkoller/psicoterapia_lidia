About me

Every person, every couple, every family carries a unique story: my role is to listen to it with care and walk together towards a new balance.

Dr. Lidia Simonetta Biacchi

I am a systemic-relational psychotherapist. I work clinically with adults, couples and families, offering personalised psychotherapy paths to address psychological difficulties — such as anxiety, stress and depression — as well as challenges linked to life experiences, family and couple dynamics, work-related issues, grief and separations.

I see clients by appointment, both online and in person at my practice in Macherio (MB). Feel free to get in touch for more information or to arrange a first introductory meeting.

  • Psychotherapist, systemic-relational approach
  • Therapy paths for adults, couples and families
  • Sessions in person in Macherio (MB) or online, by appointment

How I can help

Support for every stage of life

A psychotherapy journey can be a starting point for facing a difficult time or for getting to know yourself better. Here are some of the areas where I can support you.

Individual therapy

For dealing with anxiety, stress, difficult moments or periods of change, rebuilding confidence in yourself.

Couple therapy

To rebuild communication, work through moments of crisis, or navigate important decisions — including separations — with more awareness.

Family therapy

To face changes, conflicts between generations, loss and new phases of family life together.

Work-related issues

To manage work-related stress, career changes and difficulties in workplace relationships.

How it works

The journey, step by step

Starting a psychotherapy journey can feel like a big step: here's how it works, calmly and without rushing.

1

Write or call me

Get in touch by phone, WhatsApp or email for a first contact and to schedule an appointment, whenever suits you best.

2

First introductory meeting

We get to know each other, in person at my practice in Macherio (MB) or online: this is the time to talk about you and what brought you here.

3

A tailored path

Together we build a personalised path, with the pace and approach that work best for you.

Confidentiality and trust

A safe, welcoming space, free from judgement

Everything you share during our sessions is protected by professional confidentiality. My goal is to offer you a welcoming and respectful environment, where you can feel free to express your thoughts and emotions at your own pace, without fear of being judged.
